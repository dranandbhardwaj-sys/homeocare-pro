📚 Phatak Repertory Study

Based on the alphabetical study approach of Dr. S. R. Phatak's Concise Repertory. Educational reference only.

📝 Repertory Study Method

  1. Record the patient's exact symptom language.
  2. Identify location, sensation, modalities and concomitants.
  3. Search the exact rubric in the selected book edition.
  4. Verify rubric wording, remedy abbreviations and grades.
  5. Compare candidates in Materia Medica and document uncertainty.

⚠️ Clinical Safety

This section is for educational study, not diagnosis or treatment recommendations. Do not delay evidence-based care or urgent referral for serious symptoms.
