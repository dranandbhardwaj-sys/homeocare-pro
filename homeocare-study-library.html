Repertory study workflow

  1. Record exact patient language, onset, location, sensation, modalities, concomitants and general symptoms.
  2. Use the repertory index to find exact rubric wording; note chapter, grade and edition.
  3. Select characteristic rubrics thoughtfully; avoid over-weighting common symptoms.
  4. Compare candidates in Materia Medica; document source and uncertainty.

Materia Medica reading template

For each remedy record: source/name • preparation • characteristic mental/general/local symptoms • modalities • concomitants • relationships • differential comparison • clinical limits • textbook citation. Compare 2–3 remedies and verify exact source wording.

Homoeopathic Pharmacy syllabus

Sources

Vegetable, animal, mineral, chemical, nosode and sarcode; identity and quality control.

Preparations

Mother tinctures, solutions, triturations, dilutions and potentisation concepts per pharmacopoeia.

Scales

Decimal X/D, centesimal C/CH, millesimal LM/Q; compare notation and official preparation methods.

Quality & safety

Authentication, contamination, labeling, storage, batch records, pharmacopoeial standards and adverse-event reporting.

Doses & Potency — educational reference

No universal dose/potency rule applies to every condition or patient. Record the selected authoritative text/pharmacopoeia, edition and context. Distinguish preparation, scale and dosage form; study repetition/follow-up as concepts, not automatic instructions. Do not infer safety from high dilution or delay effective care.
